About FoodBridge

Connecting surplus food to those who need it — bridging kindness, sustainability, and technology.

Our Story

Every year, tons of perfectly edible food goes to waste while millions go hungry. FoodBridge was created to solve this problem — by connecting donors such as hotels, restaurants, and individuals directly with NGOs that distribute food to needy communities.

Our Mission

To create a sustainable, tech-driven platform that minimizes food waste and ensures every extra meal finds a hungry plate.

Our Vision

A future where no food is wasted and no person sleeps hungry — a bridge of humanity powered by innovation.
